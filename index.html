The Problem Space

Closing the "Black Box" of Unclaimed Royalties

Live public performance royalties remain bottlenecked by analog self-reporting and statistical proxy sampling. When canonical metadata—such as ISRC, ISWC, and IPI codes—is missing or misattributed, royalties sit in unallocated holding pools for 2 to 3 years before forfeiture.

OmniPRO establishes an immutable, transparent framework that locks in data discipline at the point of performance, guaranteeing working creators receive their statutory earnings.

1

Missing Canonical Identifiers

If a single co-writer's IPI number is omitted on paper forms, distribution is indefinitely frozen.

2

Self-Reporting Friction

Artists keying manual setlists weeks post-show face strict deadlines and transcription rejections.

3

Venue Financial Privacy

Room owners resist audits requiring them to expose sensitive POS beverage or door margins.

The Solution

The Proof of Performance (PoP) Architecture

Smart contracts convert live event telemetry into an immutable cryptographic claim, establishing a single source of truth for PROs and rights administrators.

STAGE 01

Ambient Telemetry

Soundboard audio recognition matches performed works against reference databases, pre-flighting track sequences against Songview registries in real time.

STAGE 02

Dual-Party Handshake

The performer and venue manager tap NFC devices or scan mobile QR codes to co-sign the setlist, establishing an unalterable multi-signature performance proof.

STAGE 03

Automated DDEX Ingestion

Verified performance data converts directly into CWR and DDEX ERN 4.3 payloads, feeding ASCAP OnStage, BMI Live, Songtrust, and Kobalt portals.

Collaboration Over Disruption

Strengthening Industry Gatekeepers
