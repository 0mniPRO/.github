### **1. Core Mission & Philosophy**
OmniPRO is a decentralized infrastructure layer designed to eliminate the live music "black box." By replacing manual, error-prone self-reporting with ambient acoustic verification and dual-party cryptographic attestation, we establish an immutable **Proof of Performance (PoP)** ledger that seamlessly connects live venues and working artists to Performance Rights Organizations (PROs) and publishing administrators (ASCAP, BMI, Songtrust, Kobalt).

### **2. Architectural Pillars**
* **Proof-of-Performance (PoP) Oracle:** Smart contracts that enforce dual-party digital handshakes (artist + venue) to verify live sets.
* **Canonical Data Discipline:** Automatic binding of universal industry identifiers—**ISWC**, **ISRC**, and **IPI**—at the point of performance[cite: 14].
* **Standardized Ingestion:** Translating verified setlist payloads directly into Common Works Registration (CWR) and **DDEX** formats for friction-free royalty distribution.
* **Privacy-First Design:** Zero-Knowledge (ZK) attestation models that allow venues to prove active licensing and event compliance without exposing sensitive point-of-sale or ticketing metrics.

### **3. Ecosystem Integration**
* **For Artists:** Protect catalog ownership splits and unlock real-time live performance credits.
* **For Venues & Talent Buyers:** Participate in a transparent, artist-friendly ecosystem that puts venues on the map without compromising operational financial privacy.
* **For PROs & Administrators:** Upstream ingestion of clean, audit-ready performance data, eliminating administrative overhead and dispute backlogs[cite: 10, 16].
